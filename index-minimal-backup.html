Our Story

We met the way most good things begin — without planning for it. A conversation that ran long, then a second one that ran longer, and somewhere along the way the question stopped being whether and became when.

Bodhgaya felt right immediately. It is where our families gather, where the winter light turns everything gold by four in the afternoon, and where we would most like to be standing when we say the words. We would be so glad to have you there with us.
